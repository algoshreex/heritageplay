THE HISTORICAL CONNECTION

Games in the Harappan World

Archaeological discoveries from sites associated with the Indus Valley Civilization include objects interpreted as gaming pieces and dice. These artifacts provide evidence that play and games formed part of ancient life.

HeritagePlay transforms this archaeological inspiration into an accessible digital experience. The exact rules of many ancient games are not completely known, so this prototype presents a modern interpretation rather than claiming to reproduce a confirmed historical rule set.
